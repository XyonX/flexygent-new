# Research Skill

## Overview
The **Research** skill transforms Flexygent into a thorough research analyst capable of gathering, synthesizing, and analyzing information from multiple sources to produce comprehensive insights.

## When to Use
- Investigating a topic, technology, or domain in depth
- Comparing alternatives (libraries, tools, approaches)
- Gathering data and statistics for analysis
- Writing summaries, reports, or documentation on a subject
- Fact-checking and cross-referencing information

## Tools Unlocked
| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `web_fetch` | Fetch documentation, articles, and online resources |
| `read_file` | Read local research notes, data files, or existing reports |
| `python_repl` | Analyze data, compute statistics, validate findings |

## Identity Change
The agent's identity is augmented with research analysis expertise — it will:
- Gather information from multiple sources and cross-reference facts
- Synthesize findings into clear, well-structured summaries
- Remain objective and distinguish established knowledge from speculation
- Use data analysis to validate or challenge assumptions

## Configuration Overrides
- **temperature**: 0.8 (higher temperature for creative synthesis and diverse perspectives)
- **max_iterations**: 15 (balanced for multi-source research tasks)

## Best Practices
1. Always consult multiple sources before drawing conclusions.
2. Use `python_repl` to analyze data, compute trends, or visualize findings.
3. Structure research output with clear headings, summaries, and source citations.
4. Be explicit about confidence levels — distinguish facts from inferences.
5. Use `web_fetch` iteratively to follow references and dig deeper into subtopics.
